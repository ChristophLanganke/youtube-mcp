Privacy Policy

How youtube-mcp handles data from your Google Account.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Overview Terms of Service

1. Who is responsible

youtube-mcp is a personal, non-commercial open-source project by Christoph Langanke, reachable at christoph.langanke@googlemail.com. There is no company, no hosted service and no third-party operator behind it.

2. The essential point

youtube-mcp has no servers. It runs as a local process on your own computer, talks directly to Google’s APIs from there, and stores everything it keeps in your own home directory. The developer never receives, sees or stores your data.

3. What data is accessed

If — and only if — you choose to sign in with Google, the application requests the scopes youtube.readonly and youtube.force-ssl, and may read:

  • the list of channels your account subscribes to;
  • your own playlists and the videos in them;
  • the videos your own channel has uploaded;
  • caption tracks belonging to videos your own channel owns;
  • your channel’s title and ID, to confirm which account is signed in.

Transcripts and metadata of public videos are retrieved without any sign-in and involve no account data at all.

4. Where the data goes

ItemLocationLifetime
OAuth access & refresh token ~/.youtube-mcp/token.json on your machine, file mode 0600 Until you sign out or revoke access
Cached subscriptions and video lists ~/.youtube-mcp/cache.json on your machine Subscriptions 24 h, uploads 12 min, then refetched
Transcripts and video metadata Held in memory for the duration of a request Not written to disk

Caching exists solely to stay within the YouTube Data API’s daily quota. Nothing is transmitted to the developer or to any analytics, advertising or tracking service. No such service is integrated, and the application makes no network requests other than to Google’s and YouTube’s own endpoints.

5. Disclosure to your AI assistant

youtube-mcp is a Model Context Protocol server: its purpose is to answer questions asked by an MCP client, which is typically an AI assistant such as Claude Desktop. Whatever a tool returns — a transcript, a list of your subscriptions, your playlist contents — is therefore passed to that client, and, if the client is a cloud-based assistant, to that assistant’s provider as part of the conversation.

This is the intended function of the software rather than an incidental transfer, but you should be aware of it: the handling of that data is then governed by the privacy policy of the assistant you use, not by this one. Data is only ever passed on in response to a request you or your assistant initiate; nothing is sent proactively or in the background.

6. Google’s Limited Use requirements

youtube-mcp’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data obtained through these scopes is used only to provide the features described on the overview page, is never sold, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to others except as described in section 5 above.

The application is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Your control over the data

  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token becomes unusable immediately.
  • Sign out locally with npm run auth -- --logout, which revokes the token with Google and deletes it from disk.
  • Erase everything by deleting the folder ~/.youtube-mcp/. Since this is the only place the application stores anything, that removes all retained data.

Because no data reaches the developer, requests to access, correct or delete data held by the developer are not applicable — there is nothing on their side to act upon. You hold the only copy.

8. Children

The application is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 16.

9. Changes to this policy

If the application’s data handling changes, this page is updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes to the scopes requested would additionally require you to grant consent again.
